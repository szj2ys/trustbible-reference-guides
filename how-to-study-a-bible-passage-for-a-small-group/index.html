How to Study a Bible Passage for a Small Group

A useful small-group study gives participants a clear passage, a shared question, and enough room to compare observations.

Start with the passage

Prepare the text before preparing the application. The group can then discuss what the passage says before debating what it means for them.

A practical check

Prepare three observation questions, one meaning question, and one response question. Keep every answer tied to the wording of the passage.

Keep the reference visible

When a question needs a quick answer with its source attached, use TrustBible topic studies. The useful next step is to read the cited passage yourself and keep the wording that supports the conclusion.
