How to Study a Psalm for Personal Reflection

A psalm can be read as prayer, poetry, complaint, praise, or memory; naming the form helps the reader avoid flattening it.

Start with the passage

Notice the change in voice or mood, the image that carries the feeling, and the movement toward trust or lament.

A practical check

Write the psalm's movement in one sentence, then choose one line to pray slowly without stripping it from its poem.
